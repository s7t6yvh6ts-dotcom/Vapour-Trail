Our story

Small studio, big heart

Grace & Bloom began with a simple idea: the cards and keepsakes we give should feel as thoughtful as the people we give them to.

Every design starts as a painted flower — peonies, sweet peas, dahlias — then becomes a card, a print or a page you’ll actually want to use. We print on heavyweight, responsibly sourced paper and pack every order by hand, so it arrives looking like a gift before it’s even opened.

The journals came from real life: busy minds, messy desks, big goals. They’re the planners we wished we could find — beautiful enough to keep out, kind enough to keep using.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How long does delivery take?

Orders are packed by hand within 1–3 working days. Standard delivery follows, and orders over £40 ship free.

Can I return something?

If anything arrives damaged or isn’t right, get in touch and we’ll make it right. Personalised items are made to order, so they can’t be returned unless faulty.

How does personalisation work?

For the Birth Flower Print, send us the name and birthday after ordering. We’ll email a digital proof within 48 hours and print once you approve it.

Are the journals a medical product?

No. The planners are designed with ADHD brains in mind, using clear prompts and gentle structure, but they are not a treatment or a replacement for professional support.

What paper do you print on?

Cards use 350gsm textured card. Prints use 310gsm archival cotton rag, so colours stay soft and don’t fade quickly.

Do you do bulk or wedding orders?

Yes. Message us with the occasion and quantity and we’ll put together a custom set.
