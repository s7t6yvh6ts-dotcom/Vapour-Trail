Our story

Small studio, big heart

Grace & Bloom began with a simple idea: the cards and keepsakes we give should feel as thoughtful as the people we give them to.

Every design starts as a painted flower (peonies, sweet peas, dahlias) and becomes a card, a print or a page you’ll actually want to use. Cards are printed on 350gsm textured card, prints are giclée on 310gsm cotton rag, and every order is packed by hand so it arrives looking like a gift before it’s even opened.

The journals came from real life: busy minds, messy desks, big goals. They’re the planners we wished we could find, beautiful enough to keep out and kind enough to keep using.

Questions, answered

How much is delivery, and how long does it take?

UK delivery is £3.95 per order, and free when your basket comes to £40 or more. Orders will be packed by hand within [1–3] working days and sent with [carrier and service]. Full details are in Delivery.

Can I return something?

Yes. You can cancel within 14 days of your order arriving, for any reason, and get a refund. Personalised items are made to your details, so they can only be returned if faulty. If anything arrives damaged or faulty, tell us and we’ll put it right. See Returns & cancellations.

How does personalisation work?

For the Birth Flower Print, you send us the name and birthday after ordering. We’ll email a digital proof within 48 hours and only print once you approve it.

Are the journals a medical product?

No. The planners are designed with ADHD brains in mind, using clear prompts and gentle structure, but they are not a treatment or a replacement for professional support.

What paper do you print on?

Cards are 350gsm textured card. Prints are giclée prints on 310gsm cotton rag paper.

Do you do bulk or wedding orders?

We’re planning to. Once the shop is open, email [email@example.com] with the occasion and quantity and we’ll quote for a custom set.
