# Shopify launch: Full Bars or Chainline Cycles

Phones and bikes only. Never put anything nicotine on Shopify (see `CLAUDE.md`).

## What only Ash can do

1. **Reconnect the Shopify connector** at https://claude.ai/customize/connectors, then start a new Claude session. Connectors only load when a session starts.
2. **Pick one business to launch first.** Running two half-stocked shops costs two monthly fees and makes neither one look real. Pick the one you can get stock for this month.
3. **Sign up** through the store preview link in the Shopify card. Use your own name, email and card. The Basic plan is enough.
4. **Payments:** turn on Shopify Payments (Settings → Payments). Phones and bikes are allowed. Add your bank details and photo ID.

## What Claude does once the store is connected

- Pastes the policies from `full-bars-policies.md` or `chainline-policies.md` into Settings → Policies.
- Sets up delivery: free tracked for phones, £39 a bike to the UK mainland, and free collection for bikes.
- Deletes the preview's sample products, then creates draft products in the right types and collections.
- Switches the theme's colour scheme to dark.
- Adds the business name and address to the footer.

Products stay as **drafts** until there's real stock with real photos. Don't publish anything you can't post tomorrow.

## Before the first sale

| Job | Why | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Register with the ICO | You hold customer data, so the data protection fee applies to you | About £52 a year |
| Buy a domain | `yourshop.myshopify.com` doesn't look trustworthy | About £10 to £20 a year |
| Put a real address in the footer and policies | The law requires it for online sales | Free |
| **Phones:** sign up to CheckMEND | Check every IMEI before you buy or sell | Pay per check |
| **Phones:** join the WEEE Distributor Take-back Scheme, or offer free in-store take-back of old phones | Required for anyone selling electrical goods | Set by turnover |
| **Bikes:** sign up to BikeRegister as a trader | Check every frame number before you buy | Check their trade rates |
| Check if your council needs a second-hand dealer's licence | It's required in Scotland, and in some English areas | Varies |
| Business insurance with public and product liability | A bike or phone that fails can injure someone | Get quotes |

## Fraud on phones

Phones are the most fraud-hit thing you can sell online. Keep these on from day one:

- Shopify Payments' fraud analysis: cancel and refund any order marked **high risk**. Don't post it.
- Ship only to the card's billing address unless you've spoken to the buyer.
- Signature on delivery for every phone.
- Hold first orders over £500 until you've checked the buyer, for example with a quick phone call.
