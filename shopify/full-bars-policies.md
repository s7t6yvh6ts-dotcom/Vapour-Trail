# Full Bars: Shopify policies

Paste each section into **Settings → Policies** in Shopify. Replace everything in [square brackets]. The wording follows the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. It isn't legal advice, so have someone check it before you take real money.

## Refund policy

**Changed your mind?** You have 30 days from delivery to return your phone for any reason. The law gives you 14 days; we give you 30.

1. Email [hello@fullbars.co.uk] with your order number. We'll send you a free tracked returns label.
2. Before you post it, sign out of your Apple ID or Google account and switch off Find My. We can't refund a phone that's still locked to your account.
3. Pack it with everything it came with.

We refund you within 5 working days of the phone reaching us, back to the card you paid with. If the phone comes back damaged, or worn beyond what checking it over would cause, we may take off the drop in value, as the law allows.

**Faulty phone?** You have extra rights by law, on top of our 30 days and our warranty:

- Up to 30 days from delivery, you can reject it for a full refund.
- Up to 6 months, we'll repair or replace it. If that doesn't work, you can have a refund.
- After 6 months, you still have rights, but you may need to show the fault was there when you got it.

These phones are second-hand. Whether one is of satisfactory quality takes its age, price and listed grade into account. Each listing shows the grade and the battery health of that exact phone.

## Warranty

Every phone comes with a 12-month warranty from the day it's delivered. If a fault shows up through normal use, we'll repair it, replace it or refund you.

It doesn't cover:

- accidental damage, such as a cracked screen or liquid damage
- damage from a repair done by anyone other than us
- normal battery wear, unless battery health drops below 80% in the first 6 months

To claim, email [hello@fullbars.co.uk] with your order number and what's wrong. We pay the postage both ways.

This warranty is given by [Your Name] trading as Full Bars, [address]. It's on top of your legal rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, and doesn't affect them.

## Trade-in terms

- Your quote is held for 14 days. The final price is confirmed once we've checked the phone. If it's not as you described, we'll give you a new price. If you turn it down, we post the phone back free.
- You must be 18 or over and the legal owner of the phone. Bring or send photo ID.
- We check every phone on CheckMEND. We won't buy a phone that's reported lost or stolen, blocked by a network, still on finance, or locked to an Apple ID or Google account. If a phone shows as stolen, we tell the police and hold it for them. We don't send it back.
- Sign out of every account, switch off Find My, and remove your SIM and memory card before you send it. We wipe every phone, but your data is your responsibility until it reaches us.
- We pay by bank transfer within 2 working days of accepting the phone. We don't pay cash.

## Repair terms

- We give you a price before we start. If we find something else wrong, we ask before doing more work.
- Back up your phone first. Repairs don't normally touch your data, but we can't promise it survives.
- Every repair has a 6-month warranty, on top of your legal rights. It doesn't cover new accidental damage.
- If a repair voids a manufacturer's warranty or affects water resistance, we tell you before we start.
- If you don't collect your phone, we write to you. If it's still uncollected 3 months after that, we may sell it to cover the bill, as the Torts (Interference with Goods) Act 1977 allows.
