## Refund policy

**Bought online and changed your mind?** You have 14 days from the day you get the bike, delivered or collected, to cancel. Then you have another 14 days to send it back.

1. Email [hello@chainlinecycles.co.uk] with your order number to cancel.
2. Pack the bike back in its box. Return it to the workshop, or send it with a courier.
3. You pay for sending it back. A courier usually charges about £35 to £60 for a boxed bike.

We refund the bike and the delivery charge you paid within 14 days of the bike reaching us, or of you showing us proof you've sent it. If it comes back ridden beyond a test ride, or damaged, we may take off the drop in value, as the law allows.

**Bought in the workshop?** The law doesn't give a change-of-mind right on bikes bought in person. If you change your mind within [7] days and the bike hasn't been ridden outside, we'll still take it back for a refund.

**Faulty bike?** You have extra rights by law, wherever you bought it:

- Up to 30 days from when you get it, you can reject it for a full refund.
- Up to 6 months, we'll repair or replace it. If that doesn't work, you can have a refund.
- After 6 months, you still have rights, but you may need to show the fault was there when you got it.

These bikes are second-hand. Whether one is of satisfactory quality takes its age, price and listed condition into account. Each listing says what we've replaced.

## Warranty

Every bike comes with 3 months' warranty on parts and labour, from the day you collect it or it's delivered. If something we fitted or adjusted fails with normal use, we put it right free.

It doesn't cover:

- crash damage or misuse
- punctures
- normal wear on tyres, brake pads, chains and cables
- work done by anyone else

E-bikes: the battery is covered for the same 3 months. We test every battery and list its health.

## E-bike rules

Every e-bike we sell is road-legal in Great Britain as an EAPC (electrically assisted pedal cycle):

- motor no more than 250 watts
- assist cuts out at 15.5 mph
- the motor only helps while you pedal (walk-assist up to 3.7 mph is allowed)
- you must be 14 or over to ride one on the road

We don't sell, fit or advise on anything that removes these limits. A derestricted e-bike counts as a motorbike in law, and needs registration, tax, insurance and a licence.

## Selling us your bike

- You must be 18 or over and the legal owner. Bring photo ID and proof of ownership if you have it (receipt, BikeRegister record).
- We check every frame number on BikeRegister. If a bike shows as stolen, we tell the police and hold it for them. We don't give it back.
- We pay by bank transfer once we've checked the bike. We don't pay cash.
