Our 12-month warranty

Every phone, for 12 months from the day it’s delivered. Here’s exactly what that means, without the paragraph of exclusions in grey text.

Last updated October 2026

Not trading yet. Full Bars is a preview and isn’t selling phones yet. This page sets out the warranty every phone will carry when we open.

This warranty is given by [Company name], trading as Full Bars, [Registered business address] (email hello@fullbars.co.uk). It covers phones bought from us and lasts for 12 months from the day your phone is delivered. It is on top of your Consumer Rights Act 2015 rights, not instead of them.

What’s covered

  • Any function that stops working properly within 12 months and isn’t down to damage, liquid or a repair by someone else: screen, touch, cameras, speakers, microphones, buttons, charging, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile connection, Face ID or fingerprint, and sensors
  • The battery: if its health drops below 80% within 12 months, we replace it
  • Any part we have replaced
  • The charging cable supplied with your phone

What’s not covered

  • Physical damage: drops, cracks and dents
  • Liquid damage (water resistance can’t be guaranteed on any pre-owned phone)
  • Anything opened or repaired by someone other than us
  • Software problems, such as apps, settings or unofficial software
  • Normal wear to the outside, and consumables such as screen protectors
  • Loss or theft

We’ll tell you straight if a problem is one of these, and usually we’ll still offer to fix it at cost.

How a claim works

  1. Email hello@fullbars.co.uk with your order reference and what’s wrong. Photos or a short video help.
  2. We reply within one working day. If the fault is one we’re responsible for under the Consumer Rights Act (and any fault reported within six months of delivery is presumed to be), or it’s within 30 days of delivery, we send you a free label and pay postage both ways. For other warranty claims after 30 days, you pay to post it to us and we pay for the return.
  3. Before you send it, back it up, sign out of your accounts (Find My on an iPhone, your Google or Samsung account on Android) and reset it.
  4. We repair it first. If we can’t, we replace it with the same or better. If we can’t do that, we refund you.

Turnaround is usually three to five working days from the day it arrives.

Your legal rights come first

This warranty is on top of your Consumer Rights Act 2015 rights, and it doesn’t reduce them. A pre-owned phone must still be of satisfactory quality (taking into account its age, grade and price), fit for purpose and as described. In short:

  • Within 30 days of delivery, you can reject a faulty phone for a full refund (the short-term right to reject).
  • After 30 days, we get one chance to repair or replace it. If that doesn’t fix it, you can reject it for a refund (which, after six months, may be reduced to reflect the use you’ve had) or keep it with a price reduction.
  • Within six months of delivery, a fault is assumed to have been there when it arrived unless we can show otherwise. After six months, it’s for you to show the fault was there on delivery.
  • You can make a claim for up to six years after delivery (five years in Scotland).

For impartial advice, contact the Citizens Advice consumer helpline on 0808 223 1133.
