Cookie policy

Last updated October 2026

Not trading yet. Full Bars is a preview. This page describes the site as it is now, and how checkout will work when we open.

This website doesn’t set any cookies, and we don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The trade-in quote tool runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing.

Our typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address as part of that request, as with any web request. Google Fonts doesn’t set cookies.

When we open, if you pay through a “Buy now” link, you’ll be taken to a checkout page run by Stripe, which uses its own cookies for security and fraud prevention. Stripe’s own privacy and cookie policies apply there.

If we ever add cookies that aren’t strictly necessary, we’ll ask for your consent first and update this page.
