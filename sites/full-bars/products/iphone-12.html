What ‘refurbished’ means here

Is the iPhone 12 right for you?

Best for: First-time iPhone owners and anyone who wants a modern flat-edged iPhone with 5G and MagSafe for the lowest price we sell. Major iOS updates are expected to end around 2026–2027, so if it needs to last four years or more (for a teenager, say), look at the iPhone 14 or later.

Against the iPhone 13, you give up about two hours of battery life (Apple’s figures: 17 against 19 hours) and the newer A15 chip, in exchange for a lower price. The iPhone 12 Pro has the same battery and chip, and adds a 2x telephoto and LiDAR. If you want USB-C, the lowest-cost option is the iPhone 15.

Battery life and software updates

Apple rates the iPhone 12 at up to 17 hours of video playback when new. At our minimum battery health of 85%, expect roughly 14 hours, and more if your phone’s battery is healthier. Apple doesn’t publish end dates for updates, but iPhones have typically had major iOS updates for six to seven years after launch, then security fixes for a while longer. For a 2020 model, that points to major updates until around 2026–2027.

iPhone 12 questions

Is the iPhone 12 still worth buying in 2026?

For light to moderate use, yes: it has 5G and MagSafe, a sharp OLED screen and a good main camera. It’s the oldest model we sell, though, so it will stop getting major iOS updates sooner than newer models. If you want a phone for four years or more, look at the iPhone 14 or later.

Does the iPhone 12 work with MagSafe chargers?

Yes. The iPhone 12 was the first iPhone with MagSafe, so magnetic chargers, wallets and cases snap on. It also charges on any Qi wireless charger.
