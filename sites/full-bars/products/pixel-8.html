What ‘refurbished’ means here

Is the Google Pixel 8 right for you?

Best for: People who want clean Android, a camera that makes every shot look good with no effort, and a phone that will keep getting updates for years.

Against the Galaxy S23, the Pixel 8 has a bigger 4,575mAh battery, a slightly larger 6.2-inch screen and seven years of updates from launch, while the S23 adds a 3x telephoto and a faster chip for games. Coming from an iPhone? The iPhone 15 is the closest match for size and features, and the iPhone 13 costs a little less.

Battery life and software updates

Google rates the Pixel 8 at over 24 hours of typical use when new. At our minimum battery health of 85%, expect roughly 20 hours, and more if your phone’s battery is healthier. Google promises seven years of Android and security updates for the Pixel 8, counted from its launch in October 2023, so it should be supported until around October 2030: one of the longest update promises of any phone we sell.

Pixel 8 questions

How long will the Pixel 8 get updates?

Google promises seven years of Android OS and security updates for the Pixel 8, counted from its launch in October 2023. That means updates until around October 2030, one of the longest promises of any phone.

Does the Pixel 8 have wireless charging?

Yes. It charges wirelessly on any Qi charger, and faster on Google’s own Pixel Stand. Wired, it fast-charges over USB-C with a suitable USB-C power adapter.
