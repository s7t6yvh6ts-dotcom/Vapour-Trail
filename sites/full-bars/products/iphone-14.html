What ‘refurbished’ means here

Is the iPhone 14 right for you?

Best for: People who want safety features like Crash Detection and satellite SOS in a reliable, mid-priced iPhone.

Inside it’s close to the iPhone 13, with the same A15 chip but a 5-core GPU, an autofocus front camera and Crash Detection. The iPhone 15 adds USB-C, the Dynamic Island and a 48MP camera. For a bigger screen and battery, look at the iPhone 14 Plus.

Battery life and software updates

Apple rates the iPhone 14 at up to 20 hours of video playback when new. At our minimum battery health of 85%, expect roughly 17 hours, and more if your phone’s battery is healthier. Apple doesn’t publish end dates for updates, but iPhones have typically had major iOS updates for six to seven years after launch, then security fixes for a while longer. For a 2022 model, that points to major updates until around 2028–2029.

iPhone 14 questions

What is Emergency SOS via satellite?

If you need the emergency services and have no mobile signal or Wi-Fi, the iPhone 14 can text them via satellite. It works outdoors with a clear view of the sky, and it’s available in the UK.

Does the iPhone 14 have USB-C?

No, the iPhone 14 uses Lightning. USB-C arrived with the iPhone 15. Every phone we sell comes with a new charging cable that fits it.
