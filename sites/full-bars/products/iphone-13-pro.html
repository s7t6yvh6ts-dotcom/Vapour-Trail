What ‘refurbished’ means here

Is the iPhone 13 Pro right for you?

Best for: Photographers who want a telephoto and macro camera in a compact, premium phone without paying for a newer Pro.

Over the iPhone 13, you get a 3x telephoto, macro, ProMotion and three more hours of battery. The iPhone 14 Pro adds the Dynamic Island, an always-on display and a 48MP main camera. For the same cameras with a bigger screen and battery, the iPhone 13 Pro Max is the one.

Battery life and software updates

Apple rates the iPhone 13 Pro at up to 22 hours of video playback when new. At our minimum battery health of 85%, expect roughly 19 hours, and more if your phone’s battery is healthier. Apple doesn’t publish end dates for updates, but iPhones have typically had major iOS updates for six to seven years after launch, then security fixes for a while longer. For a 2021 model, that points to major updates until around 2027–2028.

iPhone 13 Pro questions

What is ProMotion?

ProMotion lets the screen refresh up to 120 times a second instead of 60, so scrolling and animations look noticeably smoother. It slows down when nothing is moving to save battery.

Can the iPhone 13 Pro take macro photos?

Yes. Its Ultra Wide camera can focus as close as 2cm, and the camera switches to macro automatically when you get close to a subject.
