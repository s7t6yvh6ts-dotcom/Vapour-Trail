What ‘refurbished’ means here

Is the iPhone 15 right for you?

Best for: Most people. If you’re not sure which iPhone to buy, this is the safe choice: USB-C, a 48MP camera and years of software support ahead.

Against the iPhone 14, you get USB-C, the Dynamic Island, a 48MP main camera with a 2x option and the faster A16 chip. The iPhone 16 adds Apple Intelligence, Camera Control and two more hours of battery. For the same features with a bigger screen and battery, see the iPhone 15 Plus.

Battery life and software updates

Apple rates the iPhone 15 at up to 20 hours of video playback when new. At our minimum battery health of 85%, expect roughly 17 hours, and more if your phone’s battery is healthier. Apple doesn’t publish end dates for updates, but iPhones have typically had major iOS updates for six to seven years after launch, then security fixes for a while longer. For a 2023 model, that points to major updates until around 2029–2030.

iPhone 15 questions

Does the iPhone 15 support Apple Intelligence?

No. Apple Intelligence needs an iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max or any iPhone 16 model (or newer). The iPhone 15 still gets iOS updates, and every other feature works as normal.

Is the iPhone 15’s USB-C port fast?

It charges at normal speeds, but data transfers run at USB 2 speed (up to 480Mb/s), the same as Lightning. That only matters if you often copy large video files to a computer; the 15 Pro models support faster USB 3.
