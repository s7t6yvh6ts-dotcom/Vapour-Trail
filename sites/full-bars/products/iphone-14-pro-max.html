What ‘refurbished’ means here

Is the iPhone 14 Pro Max right for you?

Best for: Video makers and heavy users who want a big always-on screen, a 48MP camera and long battery life.

It has the same cameras as the iPhone 14 Pro, with a 6.7-inch screen and six more hours of video playback (29 against 23). The iPhone 15 Pro Max has the same battery figure and adds a 5x telephoto, USB-C and titanium. The iPhone 15 Plus is a lighter big-screen option without the telephoto.

Battery life and software updates

Apple rates the iPhone 14 Pro Max at up to 29 hours of video playback when new. At our minimum battery health of 85%, expect roughly 25 hours, and more if your phone’s battery is healthier. Apple doesn’t publish end dates for updates, but iPhones have typically had major iOS updates for six to seven years after launch, then security fixes for a while longer. For a 2022 model, that points to major updates until around 2028–2029.

iPhone 14 Pro Max questions

Is the iPhone 14 Pro Max good for video?

Very. It records 4K at up to 60 fps, has Action mode for steady handheld footage and Cinematic mode in 4K, and the 48MP main camera captures plenty of detail.

Does the iPhone 14 Pro Max use USB-C?

No, it uses Lightning. If you want USB-C with a big screen and Pro cameras, look at the iPhone 15 Pro Max or 16 Pro Max.
