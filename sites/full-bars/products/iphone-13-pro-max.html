What ‘refurbished’ means here

Is the iPhone 13 Pro Max right for you?

Best for: Anyone who wants a big ProMotion screen, Pro cameras and excellent battery life for less than a newer Pro Max.

It shares its cameras and chip with the iPhone 13 Pro but adds a 6.7-inch screen and six more hours of video playback (28 against 22). The iPhone 14 Plus has a similar-size screen and is much lighter, but has no telephoto or ProMotion. The iPhone 14 Pro Max adds the Dynamic Island and a 48MP camera.

Battery life and software updates

Apple rates the iPhone 13 Pro Max at up to 28 hours of video playback when new. At our minimum battery health of 85%, expect roughly 24 hours, and more if your phone’s battery is healthier. Apple doesn’t publish end dates for updates, but iPhones have typically had major iOS updates for six to seven years after launch, then security fixes for a while longer. For a 2021 model, that points to major updates until around 2027–2028.

iPhone 13 Pro Max questions

How heavy is the iPhone 13 Pro Max?

It weighs 238g, one of the heaviest iPhones. A case with a good grip helps, and if weight matters to you, the iPhone 14 Plus (203g) and 15 Plus (201g) are much lighter.

Does the iPhone 13 Pro Max have the same cameras as the 13 Pro?

Yes. The 13 Pro and 13 Pro Max have identical cameras, including the 3x telephoto and macro. The Max just has a bigger screen and battery.
