What ‘refurbished’ means here

Is the iPhone 16 Pro Max right for you?

Best for: Heavy users, travellers and video makers who want the biggest screen and the longest battery life of any iPhone we sell.

It has the same cameras as the iPhone 16 Pro, with a 6.9-inch screen and six more hours of video playback (33 against 27). The iPhone 15 Pro Max also has a 5x telephoto but a smaller screen and four hours less battery. For a big screen at a much lower price, see the iPhone 15 Plus.

Battery life and software updates

Apple rates the iPhone 16 Pro Max at up to 33 hours of video playback when new. At our minimum battery health of 85%, expect roughly 28 hours, and more if your phone’s battery is healthier. Apple doesn’t publish end dates for updates, but iPhones have typically had major iOS updates for six to seven years after launch, then security fixes for a while longer. For a 2024 model, that points to major updates until around 2030–2031.

iPhone 16 Pro Max questions

How long does the iPhone 16 Pro Max battery last?

Apple quotes up to 33 hours of video playback when new. With battery health of 85% or more, expect roughly 28 hours, which is comfortably more than a full day of heavy use for most people.

Is the iPhone 16 Pro Max too big for one hand?

For most people, yes: it’s a 6.9-inch phone weighing 227g. If you want the same cameras in a smaller size, the iPhone 16 Pro has them in a 6.3-inch body.
