About Full Bars

A small workshop run by people who fix phones, not a warehouse that swaps batteries.

What we actually do

We buy phones from people who’ve finished with them: iPhones, Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel. Most need nothing but a clean and a test. Some need a battery or a screen, and we fit those ourselves. A phone that can’t be brought up to our standard isn’t sold.

Every phone goes through the same checklist and gets graded by the same published standard. The battery figure we give you is that phone’s.

Why it’s set up like this

Because the things people hate about refurbished phones (the trade-in quote that drops when the parcel lands, the “80%+” battery that’s 81%, the photo that doesn’t show the dent) are all avoidable. It just takes someone deciding not to do them.

What we promise

  • Every IMEI checked on CheckMEND, before we buy and before we sell, and never altered
  • Battery health of at least 85% on every phone, 90%+ on Excellent
  • Grades with exact, published definitions, and any replaced parts disclosed
  • Data wiped, every account lock removed, unlocked to every network
  • A 12-month warranty, 14 days to change your mind and a 30-day satisfaction guarantee
  • One clear price. No finance, no add-ons, no pressure

Where we are

[Town, County]. Everything is done by post, which is faster and safer than meeting in a car park. There’s no shop to visit.

The boring but important bit

We check every phone we buy or sell on CheckMEND, refuse anything activation-locked, blacklisted or reported stolen, and keep a signed ownership declaration and an ID record for every purchase. We never change a phone’s IMEI. Old batteries will go to a registered waste carrier for recycling. We don’t sell your data and we don’t send marketing you didn’t ask for.
