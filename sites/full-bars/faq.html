Questions & answers

Everything people usually ask before buying or selling a pre-owned iPhone, Galaxy or Pixel. Can’t find your answer? Ask us.

Buying

Are your phones unlocked?

Yes. Every phone we sell is unlocked and tested with a UK SIM. It will work with any UK network, including SIM-only and eSIM deals, and with local SIMs abroad.

Are they genuine phones?

Yes. Every phone is a genuine Apple iPhone, Samsung Galaxy or Google Pixel. If a part such as the screen or battery has ever been replaced, we tell you before you pay and say whether it’s a genuine part; on an iPhone it also shows in Settings > General > About > Parts and Service History. Phones with any replaced part are sold by email order only, never through a Buy now button. Full Bars is an independent business and isn’t affiliated with Apple, Samsung or Google.

Are the pictures of the actual phone?

No. Our product pictures are illustrations so you can see the model and colour at a glance, and colours are approximate. If you’d like photos of the exact phone you’re buying, ask us before you order and we’ll send them.

Is every storage, condition and colour in stock?

Not always. Every phone is a one-off, so not every storage, condition and colour is in stock at every price, including the “from” price, which is our lowest price for a model. We confirm the exact phone, its colour and its battery health by email, and if you’ve already paid and we can’t match your choice, we refund you in full.

What’s in the box?

Your phone, data-wiped and ready to set up, a new charging cable that fits it, a SIM tool and a printed copy of its test results. There’s no plug, as with a new phone from Apple, Samsung or Google: any USB-C power adapter will work.

Will it work with 5G and eSIM?

Every model we sell supports 5G and has both a nano-SIM slot and eSIM. Whether you get 5G depends on your network, your plan and coverage where you are.

Condition & battery

What do the grades mean?

Grades describe how the phone looks, not how well it works: every grade is 100% working. We publish exact definitions for the screen, the body and the minimum battery health of each grade. See our grading standard.

What battery health will my phone have?

At least 85% of its original capacity: 90% or more for Excellent and 88% or more for Very Good. If a phone we buy in is below 85%, we fit a new battery before it’s listed and tell you what kind. On an iPhone you can see the figure in Settings > Battery > Battery Health (Battery Health & Charging on iPhone 14 and earlier); on a Galaxy or Pixel we measure it with diagnostic tools and give you the figure. A non-genuine iPhone battery shows as ‘Unknown Part’ and its health reading may not be accurate, so we measure those ourselves too. Want the exact figure for any phone? Ask us before you order.

Is it water resistant?

Every model we sell was rated IP68 when new, but water resistance wears over time and can be affected by previous repairs, so we can’t guarantee it on any pre-owned phone. Liquid damage isn’t covered by our warranty.

What does ‘refurbished’ mean at Full Bars?

Every phone is function-tested against the same checklist; any part that fails is replaced (and we tell you before you pay); the battery is replaced if its health is below 85%; and the phone is cleaned, data-wiped, unlocked and has every account lock removed. Phones that can’t be brought up to that standard aren’t sold. More about how we refurbish.

Delivery, returns & warranty

How quickly will it arrive?

When we open: order and pay before 2pm on a working day and your phone will go out the same day (subject to our security checks), free, by Royal Mail Special Delivery or an equivalent service: tracked, insured and signed for. That usually means it arrives the next working day. See delivery and returns.

Can I return it if I change my mind?

Yes. You have 14 days, starting the day after delivery, to cancel for any reason (your legal right under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013), and our 30-day satisfaction guarantee means that if you’re not happy with the phone within 30 days of delivery, you can send it back for a full refund or an exchange. We send a free returns label. Please sign out of your accounts and reset the phone before you send it.

What if it develops a fault?

Every phone has a 12-month warranty, including the battery if its health drops below 80%. We repair it first; if we can’t, we replace it; if we can’t do that, we refund you. This is on top of your Consumer Rights Act 2015 rights, which our warranty doesn’t reduce.

Paying

How do I pay?

Once we open, by card through a secure Stripe payment page; we’ll never see or store your card details. If you order by email, we’ll reply with a payment link once we’ve confirmed your exact phone, its battery health and colour. Some phones will also have a Buy now button: those have all original manufacturer parts, and we’ll confirm the exact battery health and colour by email within one working day of payment, with a full refund if we can’t match your choice. We’re not taking payments yet.

Do you offer finance or pay monthly?

No. We don’t offer credit, finance, pay monthly or buy now, pay later plans of any kind. You pay the full price at checkout.

Selling to us

Which phones do you buy?

The instant quote tool gives guide prices for the iPhone 13, iPhone 13 Pro, iPhone 14, iPhone 14 Pro, Samsung Galaxy S23 and Google Pixel 8, in most conditions, including cracked screens. When trade-ins open, we’ll quote other iPhone, Galaxy and Pixel models by email. Get a quote.

Is the online quote what you’ll pay?

Yes, if the phone matches your answers. When trade-ins open, the quote will be provisional until we’ve inspected the phone, and it’ll hold for 14 days. If something is genuinely different, we’ll send you photos and a revised offer; if you’d rather not accept it, we’ll post the phone back free. We’ll pay by bank transfer the day it passes its checks.

Why do you need proof of ownership and photo ID?

To keep stolen phones out of the second-hand market. We check every phone’s IMEI on CheckMEND when it arrives, keep a record of who sold us each phone, and refuse any phone that fails. You don’t need to send us the IMEI: we read it from the phone ourselves.

Do you buy locked, blacklisted or financed phones?

No. We never buy a phone that’s still signed in to iCloud (Find My), a Google account (Factory Reset Protection) or a Samsung account (Reactivation Lock), one that’s reported lost or stolen or blocked by a network, or one with finance still owing, whatever the price. These are hard stops, not discounts, and we never change or interfere with a phone’s IMEI. Signing out takes minutes: see how to sign out and reset your phone. If a phone is recorded as lost or stolen on a police or insurer register, we’ll tell the police and hold it for them (for up to 28 days). If they don’t need it, or it’s blocked for another reason (such as an unpaid bill), or we can’t confirm you own it, we won’t buy it and we’ll return it to you free.
