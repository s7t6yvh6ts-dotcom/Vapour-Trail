Every phone, checked properly

Buying second-hand shouldn’t feel like a gamble. This is what happens to every phone before it reaches you.

  1. IMEI-checked on CheckMEND

    Every phone’s IMEI is checked on CheckMEND before we buy it, and again before we sell it. Activation-locked, blacklisted, reported-stolen or financed phones are refused, and we never alter an IMEI.

  2. Tested, function by function

    Screen, every camera, speakers, all microphones, every button, wired and wireless charging, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data on a live SIM, GPS, NFC, Face ID or fingerprint, sensors and haptics. The results are logged and kept.

  3. Battery 85% or more

    We read the battery health on every phone and test it under load. Anything below 85% gets a new battery before it’s listed, and we tell you before you pay if it’s been replaced and whether it’s a genuine part. Excellent phones have 90% or more.

  4. Wiped and unlinked

    The previous owner’s data is erased, and every account lock is removed: Find My on iPhones, Google’s Factory Reset Protection and Samsung’s Reactivation Lock. The phone is factory reset after testing, ready for you.

  5. Unlocked and SIM-tested

    Every phone is tested with a UK SIM for calls and mobile data. It works with any UK network, SIM-only deal or eSIM.

  6. Graded to a published standard

    Excellent, Very Good or Good, with exact definitions for the screen, the body and the battery. If we’re not sure between two grades, it gets the lower one. If any part has ever been replaced, we say so before you pay.

Honest grades, published

Grades only describe how the phone looks. Every grade is 100% working, and we publish the minimum battery health for each. If we’re not sure, it’s the lower grade.

Common questions

All questions →
Are your phones unlocked?

Yes. Every phone we sell is unlocked and tested with a UK SIM. It will work with any UK network, including SIM-only and eSIM deals, and with local SIMs abroad.

What battery health will my phone have?

At least 85% of its original capacity: 90% or more for Excellent and 88% or more for Very Good. If a phone we buy in is below 85%, we fit a new battery before it’s listed and tell you what kind. On an iPhone you can see the figure in Settings > Battery > Battery Health; on a Galaxy or Pixel we measure it with diagnostic tools and give you the figure. A non-genuine iPhone battery shows as ‘Unknown Part’ and its health reading may not be accurate, so we measure those ourselves too. Want the exact figure for any phone? Ask when you order.

Can I return it if I change my mind?

Yes. You have 14 days, starting the day after delivery, to cancel for any reason (your legal right under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013), and our 30-day satisfaction guarantee means that if you’re not happy with the phone within 30 days of delivery, you can send it back for a full refund or an exchange. We send a free returns label. Please sign out of your accounts and reset the phone before you send it.

Which phones do you buy?

The instant quote tool gives guide prices for the iPhone 13, iPhone 13 Pro, iPhone 14, iPhone 14 Pro, Samsung Galaxy S23 and Google Pixel 8, in most conditions, including cracked screens. When trade-ins open, we’ll quote other iPhone, Galaxy and Pixel models by email. Get a quote.

Is the online quote what you’ll pay?

Yes, if the phone matches your answers. When trade-ins open, the quote will be provisional until we’ve inspected the phone, and it’ll hold for 14 days. If something is genuinely different, we’ll send you photos and a revised offer; if you’d rather not accept it, we’ll post the phone back free. We’ll pay by bank transfer the day it passes its checks.
