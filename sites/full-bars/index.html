What's the difference between refurbished and used?

A used phone is sold as it comes. A refurbished phone from us has passed a 64-point test, had any faulty parts replaced, had its battery checked and its data wiped, and then been graded.

It also comes with a 12-month warranty, which a private sale won't.

How healthy will the battery be?

Every phone has at least 85% of its original battery capacity. If a phone tests lower, we fit a new battery before it goes on sale.

Each listing shows the battery health of that exact phone.

What does the 12-month warranty cover?

Any fault that shows up through normal use in the first 12 months. We'll repair it, replace it or refund you.

It doesn't cover accidental damage, such as a cracked screen from a drop or water damage.

Is it unlocked? Can I use an eSIM?

Yes. Every phone is unlocked and works on any UK network, including EE, O2, Vodafone, Three and the smaller networks that run on them.

All the phones we sell take a nano-SIM and also support eSIM. Your network can move your number to an eSIM if you prefer.

What if I change my mind?

You have 30 days from delivery to send it back for a full refund, for any reason. The law only requires 14 days for online orders.

Get in touch and we'll send you a free returns label. We refund you within 5 working days of the phone reaching us.
