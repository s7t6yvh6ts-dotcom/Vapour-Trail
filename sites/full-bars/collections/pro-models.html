How the Pro models compare

Every Pro has a telephoto camera, LiDAR and a premium frame. The zoom grows with each generation: 2x on the iPhone 12 Pro, 3x on the 13 Pro, 14 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro, and 5x on the iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 16 Pro and iPhone 16 Pro Max. From the 13 Pro onwards every Pro has a 120Hz ProMotion screen, and from the iPhone 14 Pro an always-on display and a 48MP main camera.

If you want Apple Intelligence, choose a 15 Pro or later. If you mainly want the telephoto for photos of people and pets, an iPhone 13 Pro gives you a 3x zoom for much less. Pro Max models add a bigger screen and several more hours of battery, but weigh about 30 to 35g more.
