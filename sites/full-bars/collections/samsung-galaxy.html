Why a refurbished Galaxy S23?

The Galaxy S23 is Samsung’s compact flagship: a 6.1-inch 120Hz AMOLED screen, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy chip and a true 3x optical telephoto, in a 168g body. Samsung promised it four major Android upgrades (up to Android 17) and five years of security updates from its February 2023 launch, so security updates should continue until around early 2028.

Compared with the Pixel 8, the S23 has the telephoto lens and the faster chip for games; the Pixel has the bigger battery and the longer update promise. Weighing it up against an iPhone? The iPhone 13 and iPhone 14 sit either side of it on price.

Every Galaxy we sell has had its Google account and Samsung account removed (so Factory Reset Protection and Reactivation Lock are off), an IMEI check and our full function test, including the fingerprint sensor and every camera.
