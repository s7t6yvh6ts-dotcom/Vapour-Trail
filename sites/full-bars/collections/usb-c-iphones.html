Not all USB-C iPhones are equal

Every iPhone 15 and 16 charges over USB-C, so one cable can charge your phone, laptop, tablet and earbuds. The standard models, the iPhone 15, iPhone 15 Plus and iPhone 16, transfer data at USB 2 speed (up to 480Mb/s). The Pro models, from the iPhone 15 Pro to the iPhone 16 Pro Max, support USB 3 at up to 10Gb/s with a suitable cable, which makes a big difference if you copy long 4K videos to a computer.

Every phone we sell comes with a new charging cable, USB-C to USB-C for these models. You can also charge an iPhone 15 or 16 from most USB-C chargers and power banks you already own. Our Galaxy and Pixel phones use USB-C too.
