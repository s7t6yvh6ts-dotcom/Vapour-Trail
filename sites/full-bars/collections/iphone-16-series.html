Which iPhone 16?

Every iPhone 16 we sell supports Apple Intelligence and has Camera Control, the Action button and USB-C. The iPhone 16 uses the A18 chip with a 48MP Fusion camera and up to 22 hours of video playback when new. The iPhone 16 Pro (6.3-inch) and iPhone 16 Pro Max (6.9-inch) add the A18 Pro, an always-on 120Hz ProMotion display, a 48MP Ultra Wide and a 5x telephoto; both have the same cameras.

Battery life is where they differ most: up to 27 hours for the 16 Pro and 33 hours for the 16 Pro Max, the longest of any iPhone we sell. These are the newest iPhones we sell, so they should get iOS updates for the longest.
