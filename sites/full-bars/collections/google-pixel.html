Why a refurbished Pixel 8?

The Pixel 8 is Google’s own phone, with the Tensor G3 chip, a bright 6.2-inch 120Hz Actua display and a 50MP main camera backed by Google’s photo processing and editing tools. Its biggest strength is support: Google promises seven years of Android and security updates from its October 2023 launch, to around October 2030.

Against the Galaxy S23, the Pixel has the bigger battery (4,575mAh against 3,900mAh) and the longer update promise, while the Galaxy adds a 3x telephoto. Both are IP68 rated, charge wirelessly and use USB-C.

Every Pixel we sell has had its Google account removed (so Factory Reset Protection is off), an IMEI check and our full function test, including the fingerprint sensor and every camera.
