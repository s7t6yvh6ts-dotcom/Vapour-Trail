Plus or Pro Max?

Plus models give you the big screen and a big battery at a lower price and weight: the iPhone 14 Plus and iPhone 15 Plus both offer up to 26 hours of video playback when new and weigh around 200g. Pro Max models add a telephoto, a 120Hz ProMotion screen and even more battery: 28 hours on the iPhone 13 Pro Max, 29 on the iPhone 14 Pro Max and iPhone 15 Pro Max, and 33 on the iPhone 16 Pro Max, which also has the biggest screen at 6.9 inches.

At our minimum battery health of 85%, expect roughly 85% of those figures. If you want USB-C, choose a 15 Plus, 15 Pro Max or 16 Pro Max.
