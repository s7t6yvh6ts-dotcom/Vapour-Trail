Choosing between the iPhone 14 models

The iPhone 14 and iPhone 14 Plus are close to the iPhone 13 inside, with the A15 Bionic and a 5-core GPU, plus Crash Detection, Emergency SOS via satellite and an autofocus front camera. The Plus has a 6.7-inch screen and up to 26 hours of video playback when new, against 20 for the 14. The iPhone 14 Pro and iPhone 14 Pro Max are a bigger jump: the A16 chip, the Dynamic Island, an always-on 120Hz display and a 48MP main camera with a 3x telephoto.

All four use Lightning, and none supports Apple Intelligence. For that you need an iPhone 15 Pro or any iPhone 16 (or newer).
