Which iPhone 13 is right for you?

All four share the A15 Bionic chip, 5G and MagSafe. The iPhone 13 mini is the one to choose if you want a phone you can use one-handed, but it has the smallest battery. The iPhone 13 is the value pick: up to 19 hours of video playback when new, two more than the iPhone 12. The iPhone 13 Pro and iPhone 13 Pro Max add a 3x telephoto, macro photography and a 120Hz ProMotion screen; the Pro Max has the biggest battery in the range, up to 28 hours.

Every iPhone 13 uses Lightning rather than USB-C; if that matters, look at the iPhone 15 range. If you want Crash Detection and satellite SOS, the iPhone 14 is very similar to the 13 and adds both.
