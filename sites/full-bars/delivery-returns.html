Delivery & returns

Free, tracked and signed for, on every phone, anywhere in the UK. 14 days to change your mind, a 30-day satisfaction guarantee, and your legal rights on top.

Last updated October 2026

Not trading yet. Full Bars is a preview and isn’t sending or accepting parcels yet. This page sets out how delivery, cancellation and returns will work when we open.

How it will go out

  • Free Royal Mail Special Delivery or an equivalent service: insured, tracked and signed for at the door. We won’t use ‘leave safe’ on phones.
  • Order and pay before 2pm on a working day and it will go out the same day, subject to our security checks (we’ll review some first orders by hand before they ship). That usually means it arrives the next working day.
  • We’ll email you the tracking reference when your phone ships. We’ll deliver to UK addresses only.

The phone is our responsibility until it’s delivered to you. If it hasn’t arrived when expected, or arrives damaged, email hello@fullbars.co.uk and we’ll sort it out.

What’s in the box

The phone, a new charging cable that fits it (USB-C for every Galaxy, Pixel and iPhone 15 or later; USB-C to Lightning for iPhone 14 and earlier), a SIM tool, and a printed copy of its test results.

Changed your mind? 14 days, any reason

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you can cancel for any reason within 14 days, starting the day after your phone is delivered. Tell us, we send a prepaid label, you post it back, and we refund the full amount, including the original delivery cost, within 14 days of receiving the phone back, or of you showing us proof you’ve sent it, if that’s earlier (usually the same day).

Use it like you’d use it in a shop: switch it on, look at it, check it’s what you wanted. If it comes back with a new crack or a dent that wasn’t there when we sent it, we may deduct for that, as the law allows. We’ll show you before we do.

Our 30-day satisfaction guarantee

On top of that, if you’re not happy with your phone for any reason within 30 days of delivery, send it back for a full refund or an exchange. Same prepaid label, same refund timescale. Just tell us what wasn’t right; it helps us get better.

Not as described

If what arrives doesn’t match what we told you (the grade, the battery figure, the parts history), that’s on us. Full refund including your original postage, or an exchange, your choice, at any time.

Faulty: your legal rights

A phone must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. If it isn’t:

  • Within 30 days of delivery you can reject it for a full refund (the short-term right to reject). We pay the return postage.
  • After 30 days, we get one chance to repair or replace it; if that doesn’t fix it, you can reject it for a refund (which after six months may be reduced for the use you’ve had) or keep it at a reduced price.
  • In the first six months a fault is presumed to have been there on delivery unless we can show it wasn’t; after six months it’s for you to show it was.

Your 12-month warranty sits on top of your Consumer Rights Act 2015 rights: repair first, then replace, then refund.

Sending a phone back

  1. Email hello@fullbars.co.uk (or use the form below) to tell us.
  2. Back up the phone, sign out of your accounts and reset it before sending it (how to). If it arrives still locked to your account, we’ll contact you so you can remove it remotely.
  3. We send you a free, tracked returns label. Post the phone back within 14 days of telling us, with the cable and SIM tool, well packed.
  4. We refund you to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving it, or of you showing us proof you’ve sent it, if earlier. Usually it’s the same day it arrives.

One thing we check

Every phone we send out has its identifier recorded privately against the order (it never appears on our website, emails or receipts). When it comes back, we check it’s the same phone. That’s not about you: it’s the one thing that lets us keep returns this simple for everyone.

Selling a phone to us?

We email you a prepaid, tracked, insured label. Drop the parcel at any Post Office and keep the receipt. See sell your phone.
