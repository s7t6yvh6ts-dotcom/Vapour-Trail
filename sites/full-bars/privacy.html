Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

Not trading yet. Full Bars is a preview. Nothing on this site collects personal data yet: the forms don’t send anything. This policy sets out how we’ll handle your information when we open.

This policy explains what personal information we collect and how we use it. [Company name], trading as Full Bars ([Registered business address]), is the data controller under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). ICO registration number: [ICO registration number]. Contact us about privacy at hello@fullbars.co.uk.

What we collect

  • When you contact us: your name, email address and anything you include in your message.
  • When you buy: your name, delivery address, phone number for the courier, what you bought and payment confirmation. Card payments will be handled by Stripe; we will never see or store your card details.
  • When you sell us a phone: your name and contact details, your answers to the quote questions, proof of ownership, details from your photo ID, and your bank details so we can pay you. We read the phone’s IMEI from the phone itself when it arrives; we never ask you to send it, and we never publish it.

This website has no accounts and doesn’t store anything you type. The quote tool works in your browser and keeps your answers there for this tab only; they’re never sent to us, and closing the tab clears them. In this preview our forms don’t send anything; when we open, they’ll open your own email app and nothing is sent until you press send there.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you, and to sell, deliver and support your phone, or to buy yours (to perform our contract with you).
  • To keep sales, purchase and tax records (legal obligation).
  • To check the IMEI of every phone we buy or sell on CheckMEND, keep records of who sold each phone, and help the police if a phone turns out to be stolen (our legitimate interest in preventing crime, and legal obligations where they apply).

We don’t send marketing emails unless you ask us to, and we never sell your information.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the business:

  • our website host, which keeps technical logs including your IP address for up to [30] days for security;
  • our email provider;
  • Stripe, which will process card payments and also uses your data as an independent controller for fraud prevention;
  • Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser loads our typefaces (see our cookie policy);
  • the delivery company carrying your parcel;
  • CheckMEND (run by Recipero), which checks each phone’s IMEI against loss, theft, insurance and network-block records;
  • our accountant;
  • the police or other authorities where the law requires or allows it. If a phone sent to us is recorded as lost or stolen on a police or insurer register, we pass the phone’s details and the seller’s details to the police.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK, with appropriate safeguards in place.

How we protect device identifiers

Phone identifiers (IMEI, serial number and similar) are kept only in our private records, encrypted and with access logged. They never appear on our website, in links, in product photos or in marketing.

How long we keep it

Sales and purchase records, including trade-in records and ownership declarations, are kept for six years for tax and crime-prevention purposes. Copies of ID are deleted once we’ve recorded the details we need. General enquiries are deleted within two years.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to have it transferred. Email hello@fullbars.co.uk. If you’re unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Information Commission, the UK’s data protection regulator, still known as the ICO (ico.org.uk).
