Terms & conditions of sale

Last updated October 2026

Not trading yet. Full Bars is a preview and can’t take orders, payments or phones yet. These terms set out how we’ll trade when we open; until then they don’t apply to any sale.

These terms apply when you buy a phone from us or sell one to us. Please read them before ordering. Nothing in them affects your legal rights as a consumer.

1. Who we are

Full Bars is a trading name of [Company name], a company [registered in England and Wales], company number [Company number], registered office [Registered business address]. VAT number: [VAT number, if registered]. Email hello@fullbars.co.uk, phone [Phone number].

2. Our phones

We sell refurbished (pre-owned) iPhone, Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel phones that we have inspected, tested, data-wiped and graded, and whose IMEI we have checked on CheckMEND. They are not new. “Refurbished” means each phone has been function-tested, any failed part has been replaced, the battery has been replaced if its health was below 85%, and the phone has been cleaned, wiped, factory reset and unlocked, as described on our grading page. Each is graded Excellent, Very Good or Good under our published grading standard, which sets out what each grade allows on the screen and body and its minimum battery health (90% for Excellent, 88% for Very Good, 85% for Good). Every phone is unlocked and has had every account lock removed. If a part has been replaced, we tell you before you pay. Product pictures are illustrations, not photos of the actual phone, and colours are approximate. Colour and grade availability is subject to stock and confirmed before you pay.

3. Ordering and when the contract is made

When you send us an order by email, we reply to confirm availability, the exact phone and the total price, with a secure payment link. If you buy using a “Buy now” payment link on our website, you’ll be asked to accept these terms before paying, and your payment is an offer to buy. Buy now is only offered on phones with all original manufacturer parts. We email you within one working day of payment to confirm the exact phone, its battery health and colour; if we can’t match the phone, grade or colour you chose, we refund you in full. In both cases the contract is made when we email to confirm your order has been accepted. If we can’t supply the phone you’ve paid for, we’ll tell you and refund you in full promptly.

4. Prices and payment

Prices are in pounds sterling and include VAT where applicable. Second-hand phones may be sold under the VAT margin scheme, in which case VAT is not shown separately on your receipt. Delivery within the UK will be free, and the price you see is the total you pay. Payment will be by card through Stripe, our intended payment provider; we will never see or store your card details. We do not offer credit, finance, pay monthly or buy now, pay later.

5. Delivery

Phones will go by Royal Mail Special Delivery or an equivalent tracked, insured, signed-for service, to UK addresses only. Orders paid before 2pm on a working day will be dispatched the same day, subject to our security checks; we may review a first order by hand before it ships. The phone is our responsibility until it’s delivered. See delivery and returns.

6. Your right to cancel and our satisfaction guarantee

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days, starting the day after delivery. In addition, our 30-day satisfaction guarantee lets you return the phone within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or an exchange if you’re not happy with it. To cancel or return, tell us clearly by email (you can use the model form on our delivery and returns page), then return the phone within 14 days of telling us. We provide a free returns label. We refund the price and the original delivery cost within 14 days of receiving the phone back, or of you providing proof that you’ve returned it, if earlier. We may reduce the refund if the phone has been handled more than necessary to establish its nature, characteristics and functioning, or damaged. Please sign out of your accounts and reset the phone before returning it.

7. If something is wrong

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, the phone must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. For a pre-owned phone, satisfactory quality takes its age, grade and price into account. If it isn’t, you can reject it for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. After that you’re entitled to a repair or replacement, and if that doesn’t fix the problem, a price reduction or refund. For the first six months after delivery, a fault is presumed to have been present on delivery unless we prove otherwise. Our 12-month warranty, which includes the battery if its health falls below 80%, is on top of your Consumer Rights Act 2015 rights, not instead of them.

8. Our liability

We are responsible for loss or damage you suffer that is a foreseeable result of us breaking these terms or failing to use reasonable care and skill. We do not exclude or limit liability where it would be unlawful to do so, including for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or for fraud. We reset every phone we receive, so please back it up before sending it to us for any reason. We’re not responsible for data you haven’t backed up, except where its loss is caused by our negligence.

9. Selling your phone to us

Our online quotes are calculated from your answers and are provisional until we inspect the phone. A quote is honoured for 14 days if the phone matches your description. If it doesn’t, we send you photos and a revised offer; if you decline, we return the phone free. We pay by bank transfer once the phone has passed its checks. When you sell us a phone, you confirm that you own it, that you are 18 or over, that it is not reported lost or stolen or blocked, that no finance is owed on it, and that you have signed out of every account on it (Find My, Google account, Samsung account). We will not quote for or buy a phone that is still locked to an account, blacklisted, lost, stolen or carrying outstanding finance. We check every IMEI on CheckMEND when the phone arrives (and again before we resell it), keep records of every phone we buy and its seller, and may refuse any phone. We never change, remove or interfere with a phone’s IMEI or any other identifier. If a phone is recorded as lost or stolen on a police or insurer register, we’ll tell the police and hold it for them (for up to 28 days). If they don’t need it, or it’s blocked for another reason (such as an unpaid bill), or we can’t confirm you own it, we won’t buy it and we’ll return it to you free. Your quote is the figure our quote tool gives for your answers on the date you got it; a figure changed in an email or message is not binding.
