Buying a refurbished phone from Full Bars

Every phone we sell has been through the same checks: an IMEI check on CheckMEND, a full function test with the results logged, a battery health reading under load, a secure data wipe and removal of every account lock (Find My, Google’s Factory Reset Protection and Samsung’s Reactivation Lock). Grades describe looks only, so a Good-grade phone works exactly as well as an Excellent one.
