What our condition grades mean

What changes the price of your Samsung Galaxy S23

  • Battery health. Under 85% and we’ll fit a new one, which comes off the price. If you’re not sure of the figure, just tell us how long it lasts and we’ll check it when it arrives.
  • Network lock. Unlocked is worth around 8% more than locked. UK networks have had to sell phones unlocked since December 2021, so most are unlocked already; one bought abroad may not be.
  • Storage. The 256GB is worth more than the 128GB, but less than people usually expect.
  • Screen. A cracked screen doesn’t stop us buying it; we just price the repair in up front.
  • Google and Samsung accounts. We can’t buy a Galaxy that’s still signed in. Remove your Google account and your Samsung account before you reset it, otherwise Factory Reset Protection and Reactivation Lock lock us out.

Questions people ask

How much is my Samsung Galaxy S23 worth?

It depends on its condition and storage. The table on this page shows what we expect to pay for an unlocked Samsung Galaxy S23 with a healthy battery in each grade when trade-ins open, and the quote tool gives you a guide figure in under a minute.

Do you buy a Samsung Galaxy S23 with a cracked screen?

Yes. Tell us it’s cracked in the quote tool and we take the repair cost off the price up front, so the number you see is the number you get.

Will you lower the price when my Samsung Galaxy S23 arrives?

Not if it matches what you told us. When trade-ins open, your quote will be provisional until we inspect the phone, and it’ll hold for 14 days. If something is genuinely different from your description, we’ll send you photos and a revised offer, and you can have it posted back free if you’d rather.

How do I get paid?

By bank transfer, the day your Samsung Galaxy S23 passes its checks, once trade-ins open. We don’t do vouchers or store credit.

What do I need to do before I send my Samsung Galaxy S23?

Back it up, remove your Google account and your Samsung account (this turns off Factory Reset Protection and Reactivation Lock), take out your SIM, then factory reset it. A phone still signed in can’t be resold, so we can’t buy it until that’s done.
