What our condition grades mean

What changes the price of your iPhone 14 Pro

  • Battery health. Under 85% and we’ll fit a new one, which comes off the price. Settings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging shows the figure.
  • Network lock. Unlocked is worth around 8% more than locked. UK networks have had to sell phones unlocked since December 2021, so most are unlocked already; one bought abroad may not be.
  • Storage. The 1TB is worth more than the 128GB, but less than people usually expect.
  • Screen. A cracked screen doesn’t stop us buying it; we just price the repair in up front.
  • Activation Lock. We can’t buy an iPhone that’s still signed in to an Apple Account. Turn off Find My and sign out first (Settings > your name > Sign Out) and the quote tool will price it.

Questions people ask

How much is my iPhone 14 Pro worth?

It depends on its condition and storage. The table on this page shows what we expect to pay for an unlocked iPhone 14 Pro with a healthy battery in each grade when trade-ins open, and the quote tool gives you a guide figure in under a minute.

Do you buy an iPhone 14 Pro with a cracked screen?

Yes. Tell us it’s cracked in the quote tool and we take the repair cost off the price up front, so the number you see is the number you get.

Will you lower the price when my iPhone 14 Pro arrives?

Not if it matches what you told us. When trade-ins open, your quote will be provisional until we inspect the phone, and it’ll hold for 14 days. If something is genuinely different from your description, we’ll send you photos and a revised offer, and you can have it posted back free if you’d rather.

How do I get paid?

By bank transfer, the day your iPhone 14 Pro passes its checks, once trade-ins open. We don’t do vouchers or store credit.

What do I need to do before I send my iPhone 14 Pro?

Back it up, turn off Find My and sign out of your Apple Account, remove your SIM, and erase it with Erase All Content and Settings. A phone still signed in can’t be resold, so we can’t buy it until that’s done.
