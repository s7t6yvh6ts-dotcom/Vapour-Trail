How we grade phones

Every grade is 100% working. Cosmetic condition is the only thing that changes. The same published standard applies whether we’re buying a phone from you or selling one to you.

The grades at a glance

Excellent Cosmetic grade

Screen flawless, with no marks visible at any angle. Body near-mint: at most one micro-mark, invisible at arm’s length. Battery 90%+. All functions perfect, genuine parts.

  • 100% working: passes our full function test
  • Battery 90%+
  • 12-month warranty

Very Good Cosmetic grade

One or two light scratches on the screen, invisible when it’s on. Light marks or minor edge wear on the body, no dents. Battery 88%+. All functions perfect.

  • 100% working: passes our full function test
  • Battery 88%+
  • 12-month warranty

Good Cosmetic grade

Visible light scratches on the screen, but no cracks or display defects. Noticeable scuffs on the body; small dents possible. Battery 85%+. All functions perfect.

  • 100% working: passes our full function test
  • Battery 85%+
  • 12-month warranty

Function, every grade: all functions perfect. Excellent also means all genuine parts.

Every grade is 100% functional. Cosmetic condition is the only variable. We never sell a partly working phone as anything other than clearly labelled spares or repair, and never in this shop.

Fair is part of our published standard and is how we value well-worn phones we buy in. We don’t currently sell Fair phones here: every phone we sell is Excellent, Very Good or Good, with at least 85% battery health.

We grade conservatively

If we’re not sure whether a phone is Very Good or Good, it’s Good. A phone that looks better than its grade is a nice surprise. The reverse is a return, and we’d rather not put you through that.

Every phone passes the same tests

Before anything is graded it goes through the full checklist: screen, every camera, speakers, all microphones, every button, wired and wireless charging, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data on a live SIM, GPS, NFC, Face ID or fingerprint, battery health under load, ports, sensors, haptics, software status, parts history, a factory reset, and an IMEI check on CheckMEND.

The results are logged against the phone and kept. If anything’s ever in dispute, we can show you exactly what we tested and what it did, and you get a printed copy of the results in the box.

What ‘refurbished’ means here

“Refurbished” isn’t a protected word, so here is exactly what it means at Full Bars. Every phone we sell as refurbished has been:

  1. Function-tested against the same checklist, with the results logged and kept.
  2. Repaired where needed. Any part that fails is replaced, and we tell you before you pay what was replaced and whether it’s a genuine part.
  3. Given a new battery if its health is below 85%.
  4. Cleaned, data-wiped and factory reset after testing, with every account lock removed (Find My, Google’s Factory Reset Protection, Samsung’s Reactivation Lock).
  5. Unlocked and SIM-tested on a UK network.

Phones that can’t be brought up to that standard aren’t sold. This is the same process described in our terms of sale.

What we never sell

  • Phones with cracked screen or back glass
  • Phones with a faulty camera, speaker, button, Face ID or fingerprint sensor
  • Screens with dead pixels, burn-in or discolouration
  • Phones still locked to an account (Activation Lock, Factory Reset Protection or Reactivation Lock), reported lost or stolen, blocked by a network, or with finance owing
  • Phones with undisclosed replacement parts

Replaced parts

Some phones have had a part replaced, most often the battery or screen. If yours has, we tell you before you pay, including whether the part is genuine; on an iPhone you’ll also see it in Settings > General > About > Parts and Service History. We never hide a repair. Only phones with all-genuine parts can be graded Excellent. Phones with any replaced part are sold by email order only, so you always hear about it before paying; phones with a Buy now button have all original manufacturer parts.

Replacement batteries

If a phone’s battery health is below 85%, we fit a new battery before it’s listed. Replacement batteries are either genuine parts or good-quality third-party batteries. On an iPhone, a third-party battery shows as ‘Unknown Part’ and iOS may not show its health accurately, so for those phones we measure the battery ourselves and give you the figure. Either way, we tell you which you’re getting before you pay.

Which grade should I choose?

Excellent is best for gifts or if you’ll use the phone without a case. Very Good suits most people: the marks disappear in a case. Good gets you the most phone for your money. For more, read refurbished phone grades explained.
