Your instant quote

1Which phone is it?

Check Settings > General > About on an iPhone, or Settings > About phone on a Galaxy or Pixel, if you’re not sure of the exact model.

Not listed? See which other models we’ll buy.

2How much storage?

iPhone: Settings > General > About, under Capacity. Galaxy: Settings > Device care > Storage (Battery and device care on some versions). Pixel: Settings > Storage.

Bigger, like 512GB or 1TB? The instant quote covers 128GB and 256GB for now: see Another model?

3What’s the screen like?

Look at it switched off, under a light. That’s where marks show.

A cracked screen is fine: we fix those. We’ve taken the repair cost out of your quote, so the price already accounts for it.

4And the back and sides?

Dents, deep scratches, chipped corners.

5A few last things

Tick any that apply. Be straight with us here and the price won’t change when we inspect it. On an iPhone, battery health is in Settings > Battery > Battery Health (Battery Health & Charging on iPhone 14 and earlier), and Settings > General > About > Network Provider Lock says No SIM restrictions if it’s unlocked.

We’ll still buy it, but as a repair unit. A phone that won’t switch on is worth a fraction of a working one. The price reflects that honestly.

6Is it yours to sell?

Tick either box only if it applies. We check every phone’s IMEI on CheckMEND when it arrives, so you don’t need to send us the number.

How it will work

  1. Get your quote

    Answer the questions above. Your quote will be provisional until we’ve inspected the phone, and it’ll hold for 14 days.

  2. Email it to us

    We’ll reply within one working day with a free, tracked, insured Special Delivery label. You drop the parcel at any Post Office.

  3. We’ll check it

    Within 24 hours of it arriving: an IMEI check on CheckMEND, account locks, a full function test and the condition you described.

  4. You’ll get paid

    If it matches, we’ll pay by bank transfer the day it passes its checks. If it doesn’t, we’ll send photos and a revised offer, or post it back free if you’d rather.

What we’ll need

  • The phone signed out and reset: Find My off on an iPhone; Google account (and Samsung account) removed on Android. See how to sign out and reset.
  • Proof of ownership, such as the original receipt, your network contract or bill, or your purchase record.
  • Photo ID: a passport or driving licence. We’ll tell you how to share it securely when we accept your phone; please don’t send ID in your first email.
  • Your bank details, only once the phone has passed its checks.

You don’t need to send us the IMEI or serial number. We check the IMEI ourselves on CheckMEND when the phone arrives, and we never publish or alter it.

Phones we can’t buy

These are hard stops, not discounts. The quote tool won’t give a price for them:

  • Still signed in to iCloud (Find My / Activation Lock), a Google account (Factory Reset Protection) or a Samsung account (Reactivation Lock)
  • Reported lost or stolen, or blacklisted or barred by a network
  • Carrying outstanding finance or an unpaid contract
  • From anyone who can’t prove they own it, can’t show photo ID, or is under 18

If a phone is recorded as lost or stolen on a police or insurer register, we’ll tell the police and hold it for them (for up to 28 days). If they don’t need it, or it’s blocked for another reason (such as an unpaid bill), or we can’t confirm you own it, we won’t buy it and we’ll return it to you free.

Your data: please erase your phone before sending it. We’ll securely wipe every phone again when it arrives, whatever its condition, and email you an erasure certificate.
