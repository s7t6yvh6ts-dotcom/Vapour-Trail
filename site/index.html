    Delivery, returns and the small print

    Delivery

    UK delivery is charged per order.

    Orders go out by , to UK addresses. Unless we agree a different date with you, the law gives us 30 days from your order to deliver it.

    Changing your mind

    You can cancel any order within 14 days of the day it arrives, without giving a reason. That includes vintage.

    Tell us clearly that you’re cancelling, for example by email to , by post to or by phone on . Then send the items back within 14 days of telling us. Return postage is paid by .

    We refund the price and the standard delivery you paid within 14 days of getting the items back, or of you showing proof of posting if that comes first. We can reduce a refund if something has been worn or handled more than you’d need to try it on in a shop.

    The right to cancel doesn’t cover items made or altered to your measurements, or sealed hygiene items once you’ve unsealed them.

    Faulty or not as described

    Everything we sell, new or vintage, is covered by your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. If an item is faulty or not as described, you can reject it for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. After that you can ask for a repair or replacement, then a refund or price cut if that doesn’t fix it. We pay the postage on faulty returns.

    Vintage is second-hand and is judged that way. Faults we point out in the listing, like a cracked print, can’t be grounds for a claim later. Faults we didn’t mention are covered in the usual way.
