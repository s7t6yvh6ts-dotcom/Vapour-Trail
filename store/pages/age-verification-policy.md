# Age Verification Policy

**We do not sell to anyone under 18. No exceptions.**

[BUSINESS NAME] sells nicotine pouches, e-cigarettes and e-liquids, which are age-restricted products in the UK. It is a criminal offence to sell them to anyone under 18, and to buy them on behalf of someone under 18 (proxy purchasing).

## How we check

1. **On entry:** you must confirm you are 18 or over to view this site.
2. **At checkout:** every order is checked against an independent source using [AgeChecked / Yoti / Token of Trust]. This may use your name, address and date of birth against electoral roll and credit reference data, or a photo-ID or selfie age estimate. If the check fails, the order is cancelled and refunded in full.
3. **On delivery:** parcels are marked "Age-restricted product – 18+". Where we use an age-verified delivery service, the courier may ask for photo ID.

## What we keep

We store only the result of the check (pass or fail) and a reference number, not copies of your ID. See our Privacy Policy.

## If we suspect an under-18 or proxy purchase

We will cancel and refund the order, block the account, and may report it to Trading Standards.
