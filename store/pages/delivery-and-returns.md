## Cancelling an order

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you can cancel within **14 days** of receiving your order, for any reason, as long as the goods are **unopened and still sealed**.

**Sealed hygiene and health products can't be returned once opened.** This covers nicotine pouch tins, e-liquids and pods, unless they are faulty.

To cancel, email [EMAIL] with your order number within 14 days, then send the goods back within 14 days of telling us. You pay return postage unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong thing. We refund within 14 days of receiving the return, including standard outbound delivery.

## Faulty or wrong items

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, if something is faulty or not as described, contact us within **30 days** for a full refund, or after that for a repair or replacement. Email [EMAIL] with your order number and a photo of the problem.

## Old vapes: free take-back

We take back old vape devices and batteries for recycling, whoever made them. Email [EMAIL] for a free returns label, or drop them at your local recycling centre. **Never put vapes in household bins.** The lithium batteries cause bin-lorry fires.
